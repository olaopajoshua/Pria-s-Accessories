MAINTENANCE

Jewelry Care Guide

Our pieces are built for everyday living. Here is how to keep them sparkling for years.

Water & Shower Safe

You can wear our 18k PVD pieces in the shower, beach, and pool. Simply pat dry with a soft cloth afterward.

Perfumes & Lotions

While resistant to chemicals, apply your lotions and perfumes before putting on jewelry to preserve optical shine.

Storage in Velvet Pouch

Keep your pieces in the complimentary velvet pouch when travelling to prevent chains from tangling.

Cleaning & Polishing

Clean occasionally with warm water, mild soap, and a microfibre cloth to remove daily skin oils.
