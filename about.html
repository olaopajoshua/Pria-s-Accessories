Behind the Craft

The philosophy, material science, and design intention behind every waterproof piece we curate.

OUR GENESIS

Born from Everyday Frustration

Pria’s Accessories began with a straightforward observation: commercial high-street jewelry routinely tarnishes after only a few weeks under the humid Nigerian climate, leaving green marks on skin. Solid gold, on the other hand, carries prohibitive price points for daily exploration and versatility.

We set out to bridge this gap. By combining surgical-grade 316L stainless steel with state-of-the-art Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) gold vacuum bonding, we engineer jewelry that can accompany you through gym workouts, Lagos traffic, daily showers, and evening galas without ever losing its lustre.

"You shouldn't have to take your jewelry off to live your life. Real luxury is durable."

— Pria, Founder
THE TECHNOLOGY

Why 18k PVD Outperforms Standard Gold Plating

Standard Dipped Plating (Fails Quickly)

Conventional fashion jewelry uses cheap chemical electro-dipping over zinc alloy or brass. The microscopic gold layer rubs off within weeks when exposed to sweat, humidity, or lotion, leading to green discoloration and oxidation.

Physical Vapor Deposition (Pria's Standard)

Real 18k gold is vaporized inside a high-vacuum chamber and fused to 316L surgical steel at the atomic level. This creates a scratch-resistant, non-porous molecular bond that is 10x more resilient and completely waterproof.
